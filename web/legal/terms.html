What PayPact is

PayPact is a tool for recording shared expenses and working out who owes whom. It is a ledger and a calculator. It is not a bank or a payment processor and does not move money. When you pay a friend using UPI, bank transfer or cash, that happens outside PayPact; recording it here just updates the balance.

Groups and shared data

Anything you add to a group — expenses, comments, photos — is visible to every member of that group. Group admins can change group details and remove members. Don't add content you don't have the right to share, and don't use PayPact to harass or defraud others.

Balances are only as good as the records

Balances, simplified payment plans and exchange-rate conversions are calculated from what members enter. We take care to be accurate, but you should check amounts before you pay anyone. Exchange rates come from a third-party source and are fixed at the time an expense is entered. Settlements are permanent records; a mistaken payment is corrected by recording a reversal, not by editing history.
