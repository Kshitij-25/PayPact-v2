Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

PayPact helps friends, flatmates and travellers keep track of shared expenses. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address (from email sign-up, Google or Apple), and the profile photo you choose to add.
  • Payment details you add — your UPI ID, if you enter one, so friends can pay you. We never receive card numbers, bank passwords or UPI PINs; payments happen in your own UPI or banking app.
  • Group content — groups you create or join, their members' names, expenses (title, amount, date, category, who paid, who owes what), notes, comments, receipt photos, cover photos, settle-up records and recurring-expense templates.
  • Searchable profile — so friends can add you to a group, other signed-in users can find you by the start of your name, and by your exact email address (matched through a one-way hash; your address itself is not exposed). They see your name, photo and a masked email such as k••••@gmail.com.
  • Notifications — your notification preferences and the notifications addressed to you (kept in your inbox until you delete your account). Device notifications are generated on your device from that inbox; no device token is collected.
  • Diagnostics — crash reports (on by default, can be switched off in Settings → Privacy) and usage analytics (off unless you switch them on). Neither includes the contents of your groups or expenses.

How it is used

  • To run the app: calculating balances, simplifying who owes whom, and showing each group's members what they should see.
  • To send the notifications you've asked for — new expenses, payments, reminders and the optional weekly digest.
  • To keep the service reliable and fix crashes.

Who can see what

Members of a group can see that group's expenses, balances, comments and receipts. Other people cannot. Searching for people to add shows a name and a partially hidden email address; full email addresses are not shown to other users. Your UPI ID is visible to people you settle up with.

Services we rely on

PayPact runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database, hosting, and — only if enabled — analytics and crash reporting). Photos you add (profile, group cover, receipts) are stored in the database alongside the rest of your data. Receipt scanning happens on your device; the photo is uploaded only if you attach it to an expense. When an expense is entered in a foreign currency, the currency codes (never amounts or names) are sent to a public exchange-rate service to fetch the rate.

Your choices

  • Access and export — Settings → Privacy & data → Export all data gives you a copy of everything you can see in the app.
  • Correction — edit your name, photo and UPI ID in Profile; edit or delete your own expenses.
  • Deletion — Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account removes your profile, notifications and photos and takes you out of every group. Groups where you are the only member are deleted. Expenses you added to groups with other people stay, because they are part of those people's records, but your account is no longer linked to them. You can't delete your account while you owe, or are owed, money in a group — settle up first.
  • Notifications, analytics, crash reports — each can be turned off in Settings.

Retention and security

Data is kept while your account exists and removed when you delete it (backups are purged on the provider's normal schedule). Access is restricted by security rules so that only group members can read a group's data, and data is encrypted in transit.

Children

PayPact is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app. For questions or requests, use the support contact listed on PayPact's app store page.
